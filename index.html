Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Poddar Classes.

What is the batch size at Poddar Classes?
We maintain limited batch sizes to ensure every student receives individual attention, active doubt clearing, and continuous mentoring.
How are tests and evaluations conducted?
We conduct weekly topic tests and monthly full-length papers. Detailed scorecard analysis and answer discussions follow every test.
How do I access study materials?
Students receive printed modules in class and 24/7 digital access to question banks, assignments, and revision notes through our official GitHub repository.
Are trial / demo classes available?
Yes, we offer up to 2 demo sessions for new applicants upon filling out the enquiry form.
