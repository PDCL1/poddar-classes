Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our batches, resources, and mentoring.

What is the typical batch size at Poddar Classes? ▼
We purposefully maintain small, focused batch sizes to guarantee individual mentorship, interactive class participation, and prompt doubt resolution.
How are student evaluations and tests conducted? ▼
We conduct weekly unit tests alongside comprehensive monthly mock exams. Every assessment includes itemized performance analytics and detailed paper discussions.
How do students access digital notes and assignments? ▼
Enrolled students receive printed classroom modules as well as 24/7 online access to revision notes, question banks, and DPP solutions through our official GitHub repository.
Are demo or trial sessions available for new students? ▼
Yes! Prospective students can attend up to two demo classes upon submitting the admission enquiry form.
